THE SAGA BEGINS

Hearths. Trade. Kingdoms.

A world that lives

Seven trading villages

One station per biome grows from a homestead into a stronghold: longhouses, palisades, a jarl's hall, workshops and a harbour.

A real economy

Prices follow each village's actual stock. Caravans move goods between stations, and whoever makes something sells it.

By the hearth

Tavern and mercenaries

A Bannerlord-style tavern at every level: a host behind the counter, rumours from the barmaid, beds in the loft and a captain who hires out fighters.

Beyond the palisade

Raids that burn

Raiders break walls and set houses on fire. Carpenters rebuild from the village's own wood and stone. Nothing comes from nothing.

Your own village

Plant your banner, order houses from the carpenters, take vassals, sign treaties and grow your own realm.

Sagas and dark tales

Named villagers with stories, a skald who sings of your deeds, event cards and horror tales for long nights.

Installation

Install with a mod manager (r2modman or Thunderstore Mod Manager), or manually:

DOWNLOAD COMING SOON
  1. Install BepInExPack for Valheim and Jötunn.
  2. Copy the Sagaheim folder into BepInEx/plugins.
  3. Start a new world, or load an existing one. The Meadow Village is marked on your map.

Single player and hosted games. Every player needs the mod. Dedicated servers and crossplay are not supported in 1.0.
